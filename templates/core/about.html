Crafted with Heritage

Since 1980, we've been preserving India's ancient weaving traditions while creating contemporary masterpieces for modern homes worldwide.

Our Story

In the heart of Bhadohi, India—the carpet capital of the world—our founder began with a simple vision: to bridge centuries-old craftsmanship with contemporary design sensibilities. What started as a small workshop has grown into a global destination for authentic hand-knotted rugs.

Today, we collaborate with over 200 master artisans across rural India, ensuring fair wages, sustainable practices, and the preservation of traditional techniques that have been passed down through generations.

Every KaleenBaba rug tells a story of patience, precision, and passion.
