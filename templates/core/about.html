About KaleenBaba

Over four decades of experience bringing authentic craftsmanship to your home. Our design experts help you choose the perfect carpets and rugs to match your style and space.

Our Legacy

At KaleenBaba, we bring over four decades of experience to your home or office. Founded in 1980, our journey began with a simple mission: to preserve traditional weaving techniques while creating contemporary designs that resonate with modern homes.

Our design experts work closely with master weavers from Bhadohi, Uttar Pradesh, ensuring each piece reflects the rich heritage of Indian craftsmanship. Today, we serve customers across India and internationally, bringing the warmth and beauty of handcrafted rugs to homes worldwide.
